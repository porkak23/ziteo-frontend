## Design Context

### Users
Construction professionals in Bolivia: contractors (constructores), material suppliers (proveedores), skilled tradespeople (maestros), and delivery drivers (choferes). Used in both office settings (reviewing projects, orders, analytics) and in the field (looking up contracts, ordering materials quickly). Professional users who expect efficiency and reliability from a work tool — not casual consumers.

### Brand Personality
**Moderno · Ágil · Ambicioso.** A tech startup transforming a traditional industry. The interface should feel like a high-caliber professional tool — not a generic marketplace. Closest spirit: Linear / Notion — polished, direct, clear visual personality.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Referents**: Linear / Notion — clean, strong typography, high information density without visual noise
- **Theme**: Full light + dark support, system-preference following. Both modes first-class.
- **Visual tone**: Tool-first, not marketplace-first. Less color, more hierarchy. Orange (#D94F00) as brand accent only where attention matters — CTAs, active states, key data.
- **Anti-referents**: Rappi, PedidosYa (too noisy), Mercado Libre (no character)
- **Fonts**: Barlow (900/700 weight, display headlines) + Figtree (400/600/700, body and labels)
- **Primary color**: #D94F00 (orange — not the older #a43700 brown documented in ZITEO_DESIGN_MASTER.md)

### Design Principles
1. **Information first**: Data is the product. UI is the vessel.
2. **Trust through precision**: Consistent spacing, clear typography, predictable states.
3. **Orange is a tool, not decoration**: Only where attention matters — CTAs, active states, key data.
4. **Context-adaptive**: Light in the office, dark on the job site. Both modes first-class.
5. **Bolivia first**: Authentically local UX, not a Silicon Valley translation.
